FAQs

Good questions

Are there order minimums?

None on blanks. Screen printing starts at 25 pieces per design. Custom colours and private label start at 500.

Are prices with or without VAT?

Prices are shown ex VAT. VAT at 20% is added at checkout.

How long will my order take?

Blanks ship in 3 to 5 working days. Branded orders take about 10 to 12 working days after you approve the proof.

Can I see samples first?

Yes. Order any single piece, or ask for a sample pack and a physical colour card.

Do you make private label?

Yes, from 500 pieces: your fit, your colours, your neck label.

Every piece passes through our own hands before it reaches yours.
